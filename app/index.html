How this finder works. Every deal on this page was found and its price checked on 27–28 Nov 2026, at the store named on the deal or in the deal source named beside it. The discount is computed from the two prices shown; the verdict (NEW LOW, STRONG DEAL, ROUTINE SALE) says whether the price is really a bargain, with the evidence on the deal. Prices move during the sale, so confirm the price at the store before you pay. Store links open the retailer’s site. No prices are fetched from the network — every deal here is data in this site’s own files.
